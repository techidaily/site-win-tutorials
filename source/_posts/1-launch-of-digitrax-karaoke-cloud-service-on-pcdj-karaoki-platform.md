## 1. Launch of Digitrax Karaoke Cloud Service on PCDJ Karaoki Platform

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/kcstore-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/kcstore-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "kcstore-coverimage")

PCDJ Karaoki (_[karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)_) now offers in-app single song downloads of the **Karaoke Cloud** brand (_[Digitrax Entertainment](http://www.digitraxentertainment.com/)_).

![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/kclogo-new.png?fit=375%2C375&ssl=1 "kclogo-new")

**For just $1.99/song**, you get the great titles you love without a monthly subscription. This is not a rental system like the popular [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) platform, instead you **keep all the karaoke tracks you purchase and download**.

No setup required; the **Karaoke Cloud Store** is built into the [latest Karaoki update](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), with **over 3500 fantastic titles already available _right now_**. If you have **“_search store_“** enabled for Karaoke Cloud in Karaoki you’ll receive search results with a **“_KC_“** next to them, which represent Karaoke Cloud songs. Right-click and select “_purchase track_” to download for immediately in-app use.

**Featuring 20 new hits a month**, Karaoke Cloud and Karaoki are committed to providing KJs with the the hits: from classic karaoke anthems to breakout hits.

> _“We are thrilled to offer a new solution for Karaoki through the Karaoke Cloud download store. It is our aim to bring you the most extensive music library, with 20 new hits a month. KJs and DJs can now have the option to hand-select the songs they need when they need them most,” says Joe Vangieri, CEO of Karaoke Cloud._
