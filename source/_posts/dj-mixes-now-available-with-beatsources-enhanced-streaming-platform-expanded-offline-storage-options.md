## DJ Mixes Now Available with Beatsource's Enhanced Streaming Platform, Expanded Offline Storage Options

[![Beatsource LINK streaming for DJs](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/beatsourcelink-news.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/beatsourcelink-news.jpg?fit=1030%2C530&ssl=1 "Beatsource LINK streaming for DJs")

[Beatsource LINK](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) just announced they are adding 10,000 more DJ edits and increasing the offline locker to 1,000 downloads vs 100\. This announcement comes on the heels of Beatsource LINK’s new partnership with two of the industries largest distributors, EMPIRE and Create Music Group.

[![Beatsource LINK](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/BS_Announcement_-BS-Blog-Body_1080x720_final-1024x683.png?fit=300%2C200&ssl=1 "Beatsource LINK")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

This is great news for [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJs that subscribe to [Beatsource LINK](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), the in-app DJ-centric streaming service. Improve your mixes with DJ friendly edits and take up to 1,000 tracks offline to perform at venues without internet (DJs can still stream from their multi-million song catalog while you’re connected).

Included in the update are 20 years of edits from the DJcity record pool that DJs won’t find anywhere else. The edits include intros and outros, radio versions, instrumentals, acapellas, short edits, hook first versions, as well as transitions.

**From Beatsource LINK:**

> “Our DJ edits initiative is game-changing in three ways:
> 
> 1. DJs can now stream fully licensed DJ edits in their DJ software.
> 2. It will be the largest library of DJ edits available anywhere.
> 3. Artists and labels will now earn royalties when DJs play edits of their tracks.
> 
> In addition to DJ edits, Beatsource LINK has also increased the size of its offline locker from 100 to 1,000 tracks. DJs can now save ten times more music for offline play if they don’t have internet access. Both of these features are available in Beatsource LINK‘s Pro+ plan, which is currently available for a limited introductory price of $29.99 a month.”

[Read more on Beatsource.com](https://news.beatsource.com/2021/08/05/beatsource-link-adds-dj-edits-larger-offline-locker/)

Beatsource LINK is the best streaming service for open format DJs. With the latest announcement and inclusion of DJ edits and more offline support, more and more DJs will turn to the DJ-centric streaming service.
